# AZ808: The sync role isn't one of its own

Applying needs the sync role to be there, and a role of its own: not a member of the app role nor the app role a member of it (the app would read every row through it), and not the role that applies the policy or a member of it (an owner may turn the rules off). Make one for the engine: `CREATE ROLE sync_engine LOGIN REPLICATION BYPASSRLS`, then apply again. `authz.lint()` reports a membership granted since.

Reported when the policy is applied (`rowstile push`, `apply`, a migration), against the database.
