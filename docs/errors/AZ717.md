# AZ717: A share of yours is in the way

`authz.break_glass` gives its caller the relation through a share of their own that ends with the emergency access. Here the caller holds a share of that relation on the object already, one that doesn't let them in now: it starts later, or holds only while a caveat does. Breaking the glass would replace that share (a person holds one share of a relation on an object), and what it gives later would be gone when the emergency access ends. So it is refused, and the message names the share. Ask whoever manages that share, or break the glass with another relation. A share that lets them in now is kept (one that ends sooner lasts until the emergency access ends). SQLSTATE 42501; answer 403, as the SDKs do (`Refused`, with this code).

Reported to the app, by the `authz.*` functions and the policy's rules: the code is in the error's HINT (`rowstile help AZ717`), and the SDKs' errors carry it as `code`.
